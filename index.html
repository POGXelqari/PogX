About PogX

PogX is the premier competitive platform engineered specifically for content creators across Twitch, YouTube, Kick, and digital media.

Instead of relying on unpredictable platform algorithms, PogX gives creators direct control through head-to-head showdowns, clear viewer-backed milestones, and skill-based tournament ladders.

Cohort 01 Early Access: Joining the waitlist grants you priority onboarding, early API beta keys, and direct input into platform feature roadmaps.

PogX Battle Mechanics

Mechanic 01

The "Sub/Follower Goal" Battle

How it works: Two streamers enter a 1-hour live battle. Streamer A sets an engaging milestone (e.g., "If we reach €200, I will dye my hair live on stream"), while Streamer B sets theirs. The live battle progress bar reflects the exact percentage completed via viewer contributions.

✨ 100% Ethical & Service-Contract Based (Ijarah)

This shifts the model entirely away from pay-to-win manipulation. Viewers aren't paying to "defeat" an opponent; they are directly funding creative entertainment and performances from their favorite creators.

Mechanic 02

The "Subscription-Powered" Tournament Pass

How it works: Battles are 100% skill-based (gaming showdowns, rap battles, trivia clashes). Viewers cannot pay to inject points mid-match.

🛡️ €5/month Fixed Battle Pass

Operates exactly like a sports streaming subscription. Viewers gain access to premium tournament streams, exclusive live battle lobbies, weekly bracket polls, and custom creator badges without any gambling mechanics or minor exploitation.

PogX changes the paradigm. We are engineering a high-stakes arena where content creators clash in direct, skill-based challenges. Here, your craft is tested in real time, your community stands behind you, and the victor takes the ranking.

No shadowbans. No pay-to-win tricks. Just authentic talent, community-funded milestones, and verifiable triumphs.
